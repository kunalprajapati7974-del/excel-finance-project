# 📊 TCS Financial Analysis 2023-2024

## 🎯 Project Overview
In-depth Financial Analysis of
Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)
for 2023-2024 using Excel.

## 📈 Key Findings

### 💰 Revenue & Profit
| Year | Revenue | Net Profit | Margin |
|------|---------|------------|--------|
| 2024 | 2,45,315 Cr | 46,099 Cr | 19% |
| 2023 | 2,28,907 Cr | 42,303 Cr | 18% |

### 🏦 Balance Sheet
| Year | Assets | Liabilities | ROE |
|------|--------|-------------|-----|
| 2024 | 1,12,984 | 46,104 | 50.9% |
| 2023 | 1,10,270 | 43,558 | 48.2% |

## 💡 Key Insights
✅ Revenue grew 7.1% YoY
✅ Net Profit grew 8.9% YoY
✅ ROE improved from 48% to 51%
✅ Current Ratio healthy at 2.45
